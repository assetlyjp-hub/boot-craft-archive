## Overview

The Viberg Service Boot 2030 is a service boot from Viberg, made in Canada. Priced at $700, it features chromexcel leather with stitchdown construction.

Viberg's 2030 Service Boot is the gold standard of modern heritage boots. The iconic 2030 last with its slightly tapered, almond-shaped toe creates a sleek silhouette. Stitchdown construction allows for wider welting and easier resoling.

## Construction & Materials

- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Stitchdown
- **Sole**: Dainite
- **Last Shape**: 2030
- **Made In**: Canada

## Key Features

- Stitchdown construction
- 2030 last
- Dainite sole
- Unstructured toe

## Fit & Sizing

As with most stitchdown boots, we recommend trying the Viberg Service Boot 2030 in your Brannock size and potentially sizing down by half a size. The 2030 last provides a balanced fit that accommodates most foot shapes.

## Value Assessment

At $700, the Viberg Service Boot 2030 sits in the luxury price range. For a stitchdown boot made in Canada with chromexcel leather, this represents solid value in today's market.

## Verdict

The Viberg Service Boot 2030 is a worthy addition to any footwear collection. Viberg's commitment to quality materials and proven construction methods ensures this is a boot that will serve you well for years to come.
