## Overview

The Thursday Boot Co President is a service boot from Thursday Boot Co, made in Mexico. Priced at $199, it features chromexcel leather with goodyear welt construction.

The President is Thursday's plain toe counterpart to the Captain. Without the cap toe stitching, it offers a cleaner, dressier profile while maintaining the same quality construction and materials.

## Construction & Materials

- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Sole**: Dainite
- **Last Shape**: Crown
- **Made In**: Mexico

## Key Features

- Plain toe design
- Horween Chromexcel
- Dainite-style sole
- Sleeker than Captain

## Fit & Sizing

As with most goodyear welt boots, we recommend trying the Thursday Boot Co President in your Brannock size and potentially sizing down by half a size. The Crown last provides a balanced fit that accommodates most foot shapes.

## Value Assessment

At $199, the Thursday Boot Co President sits in the budget price range. For a goodyear welt boot made in Mexico with chromexcel leather, this represents solid value in today's market.

## Verdict

The Thursday Boot Co President is a worthy addition to any footwear collection. Thursday Boot Co's commitment to quality materials and proven construction methods ensures this is a boot that will serve you well for years to come.
