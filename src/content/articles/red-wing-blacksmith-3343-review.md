## Overview

The Red Wing Blacksmith 3343 is a service boot from Red Wing, made in USA. Priced at $340, it features roughout leather with goodyear welt construction.

The Blacksmith offers the heritage aesthetic of the Iron Ranger without the cap toe. Its unstructured toe box molds to your foot over time, and Copper Rough & Tough leather is among the most forgiving leathers Red Wing offers.

## Construction & Materials

- **Leather**: Roughout
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Sole**: Vibram
- **Last Shape**: 8
- **Made In**: USA

## Key Features

- Plain toe design
- Vibram 430 Mini Lug sole
- Copper Rough & Tough leather
- Unstructured toe box

## Fit & Sizing

As with most goodyear welt boots, we recommend trying the Red Wing Blacksmith 3343 in your Brannock size and potentially sizing down by half a size. The 8 last provides a balanced fit that accommodates most foot shapes.

## Value Assessment

At $340, the Red Wing Blacksmith 3343 sits in the mid price range. For a goodyear welt boot made in USA with roughout leather, this represents solid value in today's market.

## Verdict

The Red Wing Blacksmith 3343 is a worthy addition to any footwear collection. Red Wing's commitment to quality materials and proven construction methods ensures this is a boot that will serve you well for years to come.
