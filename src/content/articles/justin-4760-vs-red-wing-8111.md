## Overview

Choosing between the Justin Original Work Boots 4760 ($18000) and the Red Wing Iron Ranger 8111 ($350) is a common dilemma. Both are excellent options, but they cater to different preferences and priorities.

## Justin Original Work Boots 4760

- **Brand**: justin
- **Leather**: Full Grain Leather
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: USA
- **Price**: $18000

A dependable American work boot with J-Flex comfort system and oil-resistant outsole. The full-grain leather upper is built tough for demanding jobsite conditions. Pull-on style for quick on-and-off convenience.

## Red Wing Iron Ranger 8111

- **Brand**: Red Wing
- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: USA
- **Price**: $350

The Iron Ranger is Red Wing's most iconic heritage boot. Inspired by the iron miners of Minnesota's Mesabi Range, it features a double-layered toe cap for extra protection and Amber Harness leather that develops a rich patina over time.
