## Overview

Choosing between the Red Wing Beckman 9011 ($400) and the Crockett & Jones Islay ($650) is a common dilemma. Both are excellent options, but they cater to different preferences and priorities.

## Red Wing Beckman 9011

- **Brand**: Red Wing
- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: USA
- **Price**: $400

The Beckman bridges the gap between workwear and dress boot. Featuring Red Wing's premium Featherstone leather and a leather outsole, it brings refined elegance while maintaining the brand's rugged heritage.

## Crockett & Jones Islay

- **Brand**: Crockett & Jones
- **Leather**: Roughout
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: UK
- **Price**: $650

Made famous by Daniel Craig's James Bond in Skyfall, the Islay is Crockett & Jones' rugged country boot. Scotch grain leather and a storm welt provide excellent weather protection while maintaining a refined British aesthetic.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

### Leather Quality
Both boots use quality leather, but chromexcel and roughout offer different characteristics in terms of patina, durability, and maintenance requirements.

### Construction
Goodyear Welt vs Goodyear Welt — each method has its strengths. Consider your priorities around resolability, water resistance, and break-in comfort.

### Value
At $400 vs $650, the price difference of $250 may be a factor in your decision. Consider the long-term cost-per-wear rather than just the upfront price.

## Verdict

Both are excellent choices. The Red Wing Beckman 9011 edges ahead for those who prioritize its specific attributes, while the Crockett & Jones Islay is the better choice for those who value what it uniquely offers. You genuinely cannot go wrong with either.
