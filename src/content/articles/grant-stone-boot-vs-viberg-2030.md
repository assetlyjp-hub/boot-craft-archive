## Overview

Choosing between the Grant Stone Diesel Boot ($360) and the Viberg Service Boot 2030 ($700) is a common dilemma. Both are excellent options, but they cater to different preferences and priorities.

## Grant Stone Diesel Boot

- **Brand**: Grant Stone
- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: China
- **Price**: $360

Grant Stone's Diesel Boot offers Alden-level quality at a fraction of the price. Using genuine Horween Chromexcel and a hand-welted construction, it represents perhaps the best value in the heritage boot market.

## Viberg Service Boot 2030

- **Brand**: Viberg
- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Stitchdown
- **Made In**: Canada
- **Price**: $700

Viberg's 2030 Service Boot is the gold standard of modern heritage boots. The iconic 2030 last with its slightly tapered, almond-shaped toe creates a sleek silhouette. Stitchdown construction allows for wider welting and easier resoling.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

### Leather Quality
Both boots use quality leather, but chromexcel and chromexcel offer different characteristics in terms of patina, durability, and maintenance requirements.

### Construction
Goodyear Welt vs Stitchdown — each method has its strengths. Consider your priorities around resolability, water resistance, and break-in comfort.

### Value
At $360 vs $700, the price difference of $340 may be a factor in your decision. Consider the long-term cost-per-wear rather than just the upfront price.

## Verdict

Both are excellent choices. The Grant Stone Diesel Boot edges ahead for those who prioritize its specific attributes, while the Viberg Service Boot 2030 is the better choice for those who value what it uniquely offers. You genuinely cannot go wrong with either.
