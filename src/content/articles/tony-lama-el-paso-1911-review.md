## Overview

The Tony Lama El Paso 1911 is a western from tony-lama, made in Mexico. Priced at $45000, it features full-grain leather leather with goodyear welt construction.

A classic western boot from the legendary El Paso, Texas maker. Full-grain leather with intricate western stitching patterns and a walking heel for everyday comfort. The Cushion Comfort insole adds all-day wearability.
