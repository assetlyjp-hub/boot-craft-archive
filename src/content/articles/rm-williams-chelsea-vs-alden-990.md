## Overview

Choosing between the R.M. Williams Craftsman Chelsea ($500) and the Alden Plain Toe Blucher 990 ($800) is a common dilemma. Both are excellent options, but they cater to different preferences and priorities.

## R.M. Williams Craftsman Chelsea

- **Brand**: rm-williams
- **Leather**: Calf
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: Australia
- **Price**: $500

The R.M. Williams Craftsman is the definitive Chelsea boot, crafted from a single piece of yearling leather in Adelaide, Australia. Its whole-cut construction eliminates seams for a remarkably clean silhouette. Originally designed for the Australian outback, it has become an icon of effortless style that transitions seamlessly from ranch to restaurant.

## Alden Plain Toe Blucher 990

- **Brand**: Alden
- **Leather**: Shell Cordovan
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: USA
- **Price**: $800

The Alden 990 in Color #8 Shell Cordovan is perhaps the most coveted American dress shoe. Shell cordovan's legendary durability and mirror-like patina make this a lifetime investment piece.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

### Leather Quality
Both boots use quality leather, but calf and shell cordovan offer different characteristics in terms of patina, durability, and maintenance requirements.

### Construction
Goodyear Welt vs Goodyear Welt — each method has its strengths. Consider your priorities around resolability, water resistance, and break-in comfort.

### Value
At $500 vs $800, the price difference of $300 may be a factor in your decision. Consider the long-term cost-per-wear rather than just the upfront price.

## Verdict

Both are excellent choices. The R.M. Williams Craftsman Chelsea edges ahead for those who prioritize its specific attributes, while the Alden Plain Toe Blucher 990 is the better choice for those who value what it uniquely offers. You genuinely cannot go wrong with either.
