## Overview

The Allen Edmonds Dalton is a dress boot from Allen Edmonds, made in USA. Priced at $495, it features calf leather with goodyear welt construction.

The Dalton is Allen Edmonds' take on the classic wingtip boot. With full brogue detailing and a Dainite sole for all-weather traction, it bridges the gap between heritage workboot and dress shoe.

## Construction & Materials

- **Leather**: Calf
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Sole**: Dainite
- **Last Shape**: 511
- **Made In**: USA

## Key Features

- Wingtip boot design
- Dainite rubber sole
- Calfskin leather upper
- Brogue detailing throughout

## Fit & Sizing

As with most goodyear welt boots, we recommend trying the Allen Edmonds Dalton in your Brannock size and potentially sizing down by half a size. The 511 last provides a balanced fit that accommodates most foot shapes.

## Value Assessment

At $495, the Allen Edmonds Dalton sits in the premium price range. For a goodyear welt boot made in USA with calf leather, this represents solid value in today's market.

## Verdict

The Allen Edmonds Dalton is a worthy addition to any footwear collection. Allen Edmonds's commitment to quality materials and proven construction methods ensures this is a boot that will serve you well for years to come.
