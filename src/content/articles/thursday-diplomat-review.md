## Overview

The Thursday Boot Co Diplomat is a dress boot from Thursday Boot Co, made in Mexico. Priced at $220, it features calf leather with goodyear welt construction.

The Diplomat brings dress boot styling at Thursday's trademark value pricing. Full brogue wingtip detailing on a calfskin upper with a leather sole makes it suitable for business and formal occasions.

## Construction & Materials

- **Leather**: Calf
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Sole**: Leather
- **Last Shape**: Crown
- **Made In**: Mexico

## Key Features

- Wingtip dress boot
- Full-grain calfskin
- Leather sole
- Brogue detailing

## Fit & Sizing

As with most goodyear welt boots, we recommend trying the Thursday Boot Co Diplomat in your Brannock size and potentially sizing down by half a size. The Crown last provides a balanced fit that accommodates most foot shapes.

## Value Assessment

At $220, the Thursday Boot Co Diplomat sits in the budget price range. For a goodyear welt boot made in Mexico with calf leather, this represents solid value in today's market.

## Verdict

The Thursday Boot Co Diplomat is a worthy addition to any footwear collection. Thursday Boot Co's commitment to quality materials and proven construction methods ensures this is a boot that will serve you well for years to come.
