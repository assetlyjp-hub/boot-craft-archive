## Overview

The Church's Shannon Derby is a derby from Church's, made in UK. Priced at $580, it features calf leather with goodyear welt construction.

The Shannon is Church's quintessential English derby. With its clean lines and polished finish, it exemplifies understated British elegance. Available in various leather finishes including the distinctive Bookbinder polished leather.

## Construction & Materials

- **Leather**: Calf
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Sole**: Leather
- **Last Shape**: 103
- **Made In**: UK

## Key Features

- Bookbinder leather option
- Leather sole
- Classic English derby
- Round toe design

## Fit & Sizing

As with most goodyear welt boots, we recommend trying the Church's Shannon Derby in your Brannock size and potentially sizing down by half a size. The 103 last provides a balanced fit that accommodates most foot shapes.

## Value Assessment

At $580, the Church's Shannon Derby sits in the premium price range. For a goodyear welt boot made in UK with calf leather, this represents solid value in today's market.

## Verdict

The Church's Shannon Derby is a worthy addition to any footwear collection. Church's's commitment to quality materials and proven construction methods ensures this is a boot that will serve you well for years to come.
