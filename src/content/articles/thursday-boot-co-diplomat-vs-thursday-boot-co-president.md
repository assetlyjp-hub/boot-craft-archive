## Overview

Choosing between the Thursday Boot Co Diplomat ($220) and the Thursday Boot Co President ($199) is a common dilemma. Both are excellent options, but they cater to different preferences and priorities.

## Thursday Boot Co Diplomat

- **Brand**: Thursday Boot Co
- **Leather**: Calf
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: Mexico
- **Price**: $220

The Diplomat brings dress boot styling at Thursday's trademark value pricing. Full brogue wingtip detailing on a calfskin upper with a leather sole makes it suitable for business and formal occasions.

## Thursday Boot Co President

- **Brand**: Thursday Boot Co
- **Leather**: Chromexcel
- **Construction**: Goodyear Welt
- **Made In**: Mexico
- **Price**: $199

The President is Thursday's plain toe counterpart to the Captain. Without the cap toe stitching, it offers a cleaner, dressier profile while maintaining the same quality construction and materials.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

### Leather Quality
Both boots use quality leather, but calf and chromexcel offer different characteristics in terms of patina, durability, and maintenance requirements.

### Construction
Goodyear Welt vs Goodyear Welt — each method has its strengths. Consider your priorities around resolability, water resistance, and break-in comfort.

### Value
At $220 vs $199, the price difference of $21 may be a factor in your decision. Consider the long-term cost-per-wear rather than just the upfront price.

## Verdict

Both are excellent choices. The Thursday Boot Co Diplomat edges ahead for those who prioritize its specific attributes, while the Thursday Boot Co President is the better choice for those who value what it uniquely offers. You genuinely cannot go wrong with either.
